Duplicates usually come from the same person or company being typed in more than one way, or from combining lists. Standardising how values are written makes later matching far easier.

## What this involves

- Agreeing with you what makes two rows the same record
- Standardising names, addresses, categories and similar fields
- Listing every merge and every uncertain match for your review

We keep the rules we used in writing so the same cleanup can be repeated on future data.
