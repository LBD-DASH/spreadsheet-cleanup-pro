Cleanup is the foundation for everything else we do. Before a dashboard or a recurring report can be trusted, the underlying records have to be consistent.

## What we typically look at

- Columns that mix formats, such as dates written several different ways
- Extra spaces, odd capitalisation and inconsistent spelling of the same value
- Blank, merged or partly filled rows and columns
- Header rows that repeat or sit in the middle of the data

## How we work

We agree the scope with you first, work on a copy, and return the cleaned file together with a plain list of changes. Items we cannot resolve from the data alone come back to you as questions.
