A dashboard is only as reliable as the data under it, which is why we usually start with cleanup.

## What this involves

- Agreeing the handful of questions the dashboard must answer
- Building the views and charts around those questions
- Documenting where each figure comes from

We aim for a dashboard you can understand and check yourself, rather than a black box.
