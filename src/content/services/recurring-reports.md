Recurring reports are where cleanup pays off repeatedly, because the same rules are applied each time new data arrives.

## What this involves

- Reviewing the report you produce today and its source data
- Writing down the cleanup and summary rules
- Producing the report to those rules each period, or handing the steps to your team

Whether this is a one-off setup or an ongoing arrangement is something we agree with you in the quote.
